Plain-Language Guide to Fairness Metrics
  • Demographic Parity Difference: the difference in approval rate between groups. 0 means both groups are approved at the same rate.
  • Demographic Parity Ratio: the ratio of the lower group's approval rate to the higher group's. 1.0 is perfectly equal.
  • Equal Opportunity Difference: the difference in true positive rate (correctly approved among those who should be approved) between groups.
  • Equalized Odds Difference: the larger of the true-positive-rate gap and false-positive-rate gap between groups — a stricter combined fairness measure.
