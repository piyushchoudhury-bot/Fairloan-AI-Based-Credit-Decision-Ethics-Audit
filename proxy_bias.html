Interpretation

{{ result.interpretation }}

Why might spending/balance act as a proxy? Monthly spending, account balance, and transaction count are all correlated with income and broader socioeconomic circumstances. Because socioeconomic status itself correlates with historically protected characteristics (e.g., gender, location), a model that leans heavily on these resource features can end up reproducing group disparities even though it never sees the sensitive attribute directly — this is the essence of proxy discrimination.
Ethical Implications

Removing proxy-prone features is not automatically "the fix" — it can trade away legitimate predictive signal and reduce accuracy. This experiment is meant to make that fairness/performance trade-off visible and quantifiable, not to argue for one specific policy choice.
