Interpreting Disparity: Bias Terminology
Representation bias

Occurs when certain groups are under- or over-represented in the training data relative to the real population, which can skew what the model learns as "typical."

Historical bias

Arises when the data reflects past societal inequities (e.g., historically unequal access to credit), even if collected accurately.

Sampling bias

Occurs when the process used to collect the data systematically favors certain groups or situations over others.

Outcome disparity

A measurable difference in results (e.g., approval rates) between groups. It is a signal that invites investigation — not automatic proof of discrimination, since legitimate factors may also differ between groups.

FairLoan does not automatically label any statistical difference as discrimination. Disparities shown above should be investigated alongside domain context before drawing conclusions.
